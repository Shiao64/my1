Seating

  • Full Package holders can reserve tables.
  • The Twin Ronda has 8 zones; choose one.
  • Day 1: seated in your chosen zone. Days 2–3: random seating.
  • Tables are split 60% Korean / 40% overseas dancers.

Registration

  • Registration opens Monday, November 2, 2026, 12:00 Korea time (11:00 China time).
  • Register individually or as a group (2+ people; one representative registers for everyone).
  • Process: apply → review → payment → confirmed. Submitting an application is not a confirmation.
  • Leader/follower balance may limit some registrations. Spots are limited and registration may close early.
  • Chinese dancers can pay in RMB via WeChat Pay.
