The 12th International ICH Tango Festival · The 5th Chengdu ICH Tango Competition · 2027–2028 TangotoWorldCUP Chengdu (Preliminary)

Festival · April 21–26, 2027 · Chengdu

The 12th International ICH Tango Festival · The 5th Chengdu ICH Tango Competition · 2027–2028 TangotoWorldCUP Chengdu (Preliminary)

One event, 21–26 April 2027, Chengdu (Beijing time): The 12th International ICH Tango Festival, The 5th Chengdu International ICH Tango Competition on 24 April, and 2027–2028 TangotoWorldCUP Chengdu.

Register on Inndance — use a computer browser; a Chinese interface is available.

Venue

Crowne Plaza Chengdu Wuhou（成都武侯渝江皇冠假日酒店）
No. 2 Chaoyin Road, Wuhou District, Chengdu (Metro Line 9 / 17, about 100 m from the station)
Main venue: Grand Ballroom (Hall 5, 1,080 m², column-free, 5 m ceiling, theatre-style about 900 seats). International five-star hotel.
