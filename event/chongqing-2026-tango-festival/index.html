Venue

Lianhua Duoduo Jinyuan Dance Club (莲花朵朵金源舞蹈俱乐部) · Chongqing
Jinyuan Times Shopping Center, No. 368 Beibin 1st Road, Liangjiang New Area, Chongqing (两江新区北滨一路368号金源时代购物中心)
By the Jialing River, inside a large shopping complex. Upgraded extra-large dance floor with competition-grade professional wooden floor and quality surround sound.
