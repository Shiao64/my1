Schedule & info

DJs: Che Tango (Viet Nam), Lorena (Korea), Yutaka & Miki (Japan), Fiona (Hong Kong), Liu Zheng (Beijing, China), Gianluca (Korea), Odysseus Dada (Korea), Tu Duong (Toronto). Live orchestra: Cabeceo Band (Viet Nam). Performers: Angel Tango, Todo Tango, Angel & Lorena, Misun & Odysseus Dada (Korea); Yutaka & Miki (Japan).
