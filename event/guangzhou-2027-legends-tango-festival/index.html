Line-up

The calendar assigns workshops to Jesus & Iara, Martin & Ayelen, and Leandro & Makiko. The main poster prints full names and places. It does not label the other names as teachers or performers. M2, M4 and M6 say Grand Milonga & Shows and do not name the show performers.

  • Workshops named on the calendar. Jesus & Iara (main poster: Jesus Paez & Iara Duarte, Argentina); Martin & Ayelen (Martin Vicente & Ayelen Urrutia, Argentina); Leandro & Makiko (Leandro Haeder & Makiko Nishikawa, Argentina / Japan).
  • Other names on the main poster, unlabeled. Zhu Meng & Anastasiia Sedikova (Tianjin - Ukraine); Antimo Russo & Sunny Cao (Italy-Guangzhou); Kaiser Lyu & Irene Liu (Shanghai); Eugene Lam & Cor Leung (Hong Kong); Jess Wong (Hong Kong); tango untitled REDEFINE (Hong Kong).
  • DJs. M1 Leandro Haeder (Argentina); M2 DJ1 Shiro (Busan - Korea), DJ2 Sophie (Guangzhou); M3 Cor Cor (Hong Kong), printed that way and not merged with Cor Leung; M4 DJ1 tbc, DJ2 Vuk (Serbia); M5 DJ tbc; M6 Misha (Serbia).
