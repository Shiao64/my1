Schedule & info

8th Guangzhou Tango Festival, 26 Dec 2026 – 3 Jan 2027 (workshops from 26 Dec, festival milongas 31 Dec–3 Jan). Maestros Alejandro Larenas y Marisol Morales, 8 group classes (G1–G8, two a day on 26 Dec, 27 Dec, 2 Jan, 3 Jan; the final article gives 14:00 and 15:30 starts on 27 Dec; for other days the two published timetables differ: 14:00/15:30 or 15:00/16:30 starts, please confirm with the organizer). 7 milongas: 31 Dec NYE, DJ Bella; 1 Jan afternoon tea, DJ Jeanette, performance milonga, DJ Raymond; 2 Jan afternoon tea, DJ Li-Yan, gala, DJ Xiao-xin; 3 Jan afternoon tea, DJ Xingchen, farewell, DJ Marisol (afternoons 15:00–19:00, evenings 21:00–03:00). Milonga tickets (early bird before 12 Dec / regular): single afternoon RMB 150, evening 300–350; 2 milongas in one day 400/450 (500 at door); 4 milongas over two days 700/900 (1000 at door); full pass 888/1200; group full pass 800 (min. 5 people). Classes: single 260/300, 4 classes 240/260 each, 8 classes 200/240 each; privates: one teacher 1200, two teachers 1600, 3 classes 3300. Packages (early/regular): P1 1-day 2 milongas 400/450; P2 1-day classes + milonga 700/800; P3 2-day 4 milongas 700/900; P4 2-day classes + milongas 1300/1500; P5 3 afternoon milongas 360/400; P6 4 classes + 4 evening milongas 1500/1800; P7 full pass classes + milongas 1888/2400 (first 50; text says first 25 leaders and first 25 followers). Register via the form https://jsj.top/f/SlaIzg, then pay via WeChat; tel 13042001750. Refunds: full refund up to and including 12 Dec; full refund if cancelled for policy or safety reasons. Milonga venue: Old Dream, Jiangnan Grand Theatre (130 Changgang Middle Rd, Haizhu; Metro Line 8 Baogang Dadao exit C). Classes: Yijia Dance Club (3F Cuichenghui, 68 Cuibao Rd; exit D).
