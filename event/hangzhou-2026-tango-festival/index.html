Hangzhou Jinsha International Tango Festival

Jinsha Grand Theater & Shessiney Natural Cave Wine Cellar · Hangzhou, China

The autumn scenery of West Lake meets romantic tango. A high-end international tango event with milongas, a West Lake tour, a private banquet and lake-view stays.

Theme: Light Luxury International Tango Art Gala · Organized by Vamos 万姿探戈

Six milongas · Live music by the 吳詠隆 band from Taiwan · Performances by three guest couples
