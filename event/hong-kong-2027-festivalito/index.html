Festivalito de Tango en Hong Kong

Schedule & info

Registration starts 30 Nov 2026 (per the official site). A charity event co-organized by Long Focus Ltd and Benevolent Sharing Limited (學仁社, an approved charitable organisation in Hong Kong): "Tango more = Share more"; participation supports BSL's next charity program. Venues listed on the official site (page undated, may carry over from previous editions): Shiu Kin Hall, S.K.H. Tang Shiu Kin Secondary School, 9 Oi Kwan Road, Morrison Hill, Causeway Bay (opposite Queen Elizabeth Stadium); Danzstage, 20/F, 148 Electric Road, Tin Hau; SDF, 3/F, Vulcan House, 23 Leighton Road, Leighton Hill (near Times Square, Causeway Bay); Muchachos Private Kitchen (Empanada workshop), Room B, 1/F, Yiuga Factory Building, 62 Victoria Road, Kennedy Town. Recommended hotel: The Charterhouse Causeway Bay, 209-219 Wan Chai Road, Morrison Hill. Schedule, teachers and prices not yet announced.
