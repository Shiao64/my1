The first weekend

23–25 October 2026 · Shanghai

Wutong Tango Weekend held its first edition at FICS Xinhua 365: three autumn days and five milongas, with DJs from Shanghai, Hong Kong, Singapore, Beijing and Seoul. The floor was wood, there were snacks, drinks and a light dinner on Friday and Saturday, and leader, follower and experience numbers were balanced. No classes were announced. Every pass sold out.

往届回顾 · 1st edition
1st Edition · 23–25 October 2026

Three days, one floor

Friday was Sycamore Moment (金梧初遇), Saturday was Autumn Tango (秋舞情深), and Sunday closed with Sunset Cadence (暖阳终章). The banner picked up signatures, the staff kept the afternoon-to-night schedule moving, and the milonga filled the room at FICS Xinhua 365.
