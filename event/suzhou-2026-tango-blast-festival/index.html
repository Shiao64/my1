Highlights

Plump Yangcheng Lake hairy crabs guaranteed, and all the Argentine empanadas you can eat. Seven highlights of Suzhou Tango:

  1. Classes taught by masters Sebastián Arce & Anna
  2. Performances by Sebastián Arce & Anna Arce, Sergey & Anna, and Adrien & Iro
  3. Group performance by China’s Tango Blast company, with six pairs of guest teachers from China taking the stage in turn
  4. 6 themed milongas with a Suzhou flavor
  5. Renowned DJs from Korea, China and abroad at the decks
  6. Hairy crab tango party + authentic Argentine empanadas
  7. Stroll Suzhou’s classical gardens and see the best of Jiangnan
