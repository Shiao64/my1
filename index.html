关于活动About the Weekend

北京精选探戈周末是一场以阿根廷探戈为核心的国际聚会：精选的 DJ、舒适的舞池、来自世界各地的舞友，以及北京的文化与风景。第 4 届将连续五天，在使馆区的北京奥加美术馆酒店举行。Select Tango Weekend is an international Argentine tango encuentro in the heart of Beijing — hand-picked DJs, a beautiful floor, dancers from all over the world, and the culture of one of the world's great cities. The 4th edition runs for five days at the A.C. Art Museum Hotel in Beijing's embassy district.

常见问题FAQ

有着装要求吗？Is there a dress code?

建议正式着装，男士请勿穿短裤、运动鞋。Elegant milonga attire is recommended — gentlemen, please no shorts or sneakers.

四日通票包含哪些？What does the Four Pass include?

周五欢迎舞会、周六与周日的下午场和晚场、周二告别舞会，共 6 场。不含周一四合院晚宴和舞会（另加 450 元，或选 1,380 元套票）。Six milongas: the Friday welcome, Saturday and Sunday afternoon and evening milongas, and the Tuesday farewell. Monday's courtyard dinner & milonga is extra (450 RMB, or choose the 1,380 RMB package).

一日游和 City Walk 怎么报名？How do I join the Day Trip or City Walk?

两项都另外收费（一日游 450 元，City Walk 400 元），在报名表中勾选即可。Both are optional add-ons (Day Trip 450 RMB, City Walk 400 RMB) — just tick them on the registration form.

舞会提供餐饮吗？Are refreshments provided?

每场舞会提供小食与茶歇；周一四合院含晚宴。Yes — snacks and tea at every milonga, and Monday's courtyard evening includes dinner.

如何订酒店？How do I book the hotel?

酒店预订约 2026 年 12 月中旬开放，我们会统一团体预订以争取更好的房价，详情会通过邮件和社群通知。Hotel booking opens around mid-December 2026. We book as a group for better rates; details will be shared by email and in our community groups.
