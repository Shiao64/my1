About

Select Tango Weekend is an international Argentine tango encuentro in the heart of Beijing — hand-picked DJs, a beautiful floor, dancers from Korea, China and the wider region, and the culture of one of the world's great cities. The 4th edition runs for five days at the AC Embassy Hotel in Beijing's embassy district.

FAQ

Is there a dress code?

Elegant milonga attire is recommended — gentlemen, please no shorts or sneakers.

What does the Full Pass include?

Six milongas: the Friday welcome, Saturday and Sunday afternoon and evening milongas, and the Tuesday farewell. Monday's Tango Courtyard is extra (450 RMB, or choose the 1,380 RMB package).

How do I join the Day Trip or City Walk?

Both are optional add-ons (Day Trip 450 RMB, City Walk 400 RMB) — just tick them on the registration form.

Are refreshments provided?

Yes — snacks and tea at every milonga, and dinner is served at Monday's Tango Courtyard.

How do I book the hotel?

Hotel booking opens around mid-December 2026. We book as a group for better rates; details will be shared by email and in our community groups.
