Kai

Te Amo Tango Lounge

Kai Wang (王凯) was born in Taiwan, graduated from National Taipei University and studied in the US. He discovered salsa in 2005 and later fell in love with tango in Shanghai. In 2017 he founded Te Amo Tango Lounge, called "China's first tango bar" and a filming location for B for Busy (爱情神话); it moved to 583 Yuyao Road, Jing'an, in 2023. He runs Kai's Milonga every Wednesday and Sunday, and its 900th edition was held at the Paramount Ballroom on 1 Jan 2026.
