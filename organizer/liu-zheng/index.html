Liu Zheng

VTango

Liu Zheng (刘峥) has danced tango since 2006 and is one of China's first-generation tango organizers. In 2007 he founded VTango (探戈会) in Beijing, running classes, milongas and workshops with visiting teachers. In 2013 he co-founded the China Argentine Tango Association with Xu Xuanyi and launched the China qualifier of the Tango World Championship. He created Tango Holiday (2014), the Beijing Tango Open (2017) and the Beijing Tuye Tango Festival (2019). Also a long-time tango DJ, he builds on Golden Age tradition with balanced, dancer-focused energy. In 2018 he was honored by the Embassy of Argentina in China.
