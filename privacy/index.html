隐私说明

更新日期：2026 年 11 月 24 日

这份说明适用于 tangoweekend.org，包括 Asia Tango Map（亚洲探戈地图），以及用「探戈通 Tango Pass」报名的活动，例如北京精选探戈周末。

我们收集哪些资料

  • 你的邮箱：用来登录，以及寄报名、付款、入场相关的信给你。
  • 报名表上你填的资料：名字、昵称、国家、城市、微信号、社交帐号、护照英文名（需要时）、同行者的资料、留言等。
  • 付款时你回信附上的付款截图。
  • 在 Asia Tango Map 上：你订阅的城市和类别、你标记「要参加」「感兴趣」的活动、你填的手机号码（用来登录）。

用 Google 登录时

  • 我们只从 Google 拿你帐户的邮箱和名字，用来让你登录、帮你填好报名表。
  • 我们拿不到你的 Google 密码、通讯录、照片、邮件或其他资料。
  • 这些资料不会给别人，也不会用来投放广告。

用来做什么

  • 处理报名、确认付款、寄入场码、活动当天签到。
  • 回答你的问题，寄和你的报名有关的信。
  • 只有你订阅了，才会寄活动消息给你；每封信都可以退订。

谁看得到

  • 该活动的主办方和他们指派的工作人员，每个人只看得到工作需要的部分。
  • 我们不卖资料，也不把资料给广告商或其他第三方。
  • 网站和资料放在 Cloudflare 的服务器上，信件通过邮件服务寄出。

浏览器里存的东西

  • 登录后，网站会在你的浏览器里存一个登录记号，下次就不用再验证。你清除浏览器资料就会删掉它。
  • 网站没有装广告或流量追踪程序。

查看、更正或删除你的资料

写信到 info@tangoweekend.org，告诉我们你用的邮箱和要做的事，我们会处理并回信。

Privacy notice

Updated November 24, 2026

This notice covers tangoweekend.org, including Asia Tango Map and events that take registrations through Tango Pass, such as Beijing Select Tango Weekend.

What we collect

  • Your email address, to sign you in and to send you emails about your registration, payment and entry.
  • What you enter on a registration form: name, nickname, country, city, WeChat ID, social media account, passport name (when needed), details of people you register with you, and your message.
  • The payment screenshot you send in reply to our email.
  • On Asia Tango Map: the cities and kinds of events you subscribe to, the events you mark as Going or Interested, and the phone number you enter for signing in.

When you sign in with Google

  • We receive only your Google account's email address and name. We use them to sign you in and to fill in your registration form.
  • We do not receive your Google password, contacts, photos, emails or any other data.
  • We do not share this data with anyone or use it for advertising.

How we use it

  • To handle your registration, confirm your payment, send your entry code and check you in at the event.
  • To answer your questions and send emails about your registration.
  • We send event news only if you subscribe, and every email has an unsubscribe link.

Who can see it

  • The organizers of the event you register for and the staff they appoint. Each person sees only what their job needs.
  • We do not sell your data or give it to advertisers or other third parties.
  • The website and data are hosted on Cloudflare, and emails are sent through an email service.

What is stored in your browser

  • After you sign in, the site keeps a sign-in token in your browser so you do not have to verify again. Clearing your browser data removes it.
  • The site uses no advertising or tracking scripts.

See, correct or delete your data

Email info@tangoweekend.org with the address you used and what you would like us to do. We will take care of it and reply.

For the rules of using this site, see our terms of use.
